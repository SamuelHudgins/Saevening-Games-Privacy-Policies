# Marbalance VR Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction
This is our Privacy Policy. It describes how Saevening (“we”, “us”, or “our”), the developers of Marbalance VR (”Marbalance VR”, or “our game”), use, share, and protect your information when you play Marbalance VR or use any of our services (”Service”), and how you can control how that information is used or shared. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully, as you agree and consent to our data practices described below by using our Services.<br/>
​

## 2. Our Data Collection and Handling
### In Marbalance VR
We do not collect or receive any data from you while you play Marbalance VR; we do not transmit any data about you to our servers. When you play Marbalance VR for the first time, it will create the save data it needs and store it locally on your device (our game will also create save data if it cannot find it). Marbalance VR stores and processes the data it creates based on its current state. This data includes your progress in game modes, your Waypoint position data for Campaign mode, and various in-game settings you have set. Together, this is your Game Data. Marbalance VR uses your Game Data to ensure your gameplay progress or settings are not lost when you exit and return to the game.

### Through Communication
We use Gmail, Google’s email service, for communication. If you contact us by email, we may have access to your display name (if you have provided it in your email account), the email address you used to contact us, and the conversation history between you and us. We may also have access to other information you provide in the content you send us, like, for example, any files you attach in emails to us to help us fix an issue you are experiencing in Marbalance VR. Your display name, email address, and conversation history and content are collectively your Personal Information. We may use your Personal Information to:
* Answer questions or address concerns you have about our Services.
* Help us fix an issue you inform us of within our Services.
* Provide information to you that you request or agree to receive.

We do not share or sell your Personal Information to any third party, or use or disclose it for purposes other than those permitted by law.<br/><br/>

**Data Storage Location**\
Although we will have access to your Personal Information if you contact us by email, it will be processed and stored externally by Google automatically, as a result of using Gmail for sending emails. We do not store or process your Personal Information internally using any of our hardware or software, or with any other third-party service.<br/><br/>

**Data Storage Duration**\
If you contact us by email, we will retain access to your Personal Information for a short time. Unless we are required by law to keep it for a longer duration, we will delete your Personal Information from our Gmail account 30 days after your last communication with us. However, if we believe we have addressed your question or concern beforehand, we will delete your Personal Information from our account within two days. When we delete your Personal Information from our Gmail account, we will no longer be able to access it, though it may not be deleted completely from online locations. This may be the case if you are also using a Gmail account to contact us, as our deletion of your Personal Information will not delete the conversation history between you and us from your account. Google may also temporarily retain the Personal Information we delete until its automated systems clean up and permanently delete it.<br/><br/>

**Security**\
Although no security system can stop all potential security breaches, we take steps to prevent unauthorized access to your Personal Information:
* We allow only the team members of our Services who need access to your Personal Information to have access to it, so they can respond to your questions and concerns. We do not allow other users or third parties to request your Personal Information unless we are legally required to.
* We routinely check our contact history to ensure we do not have access to your Personal Information longer than necessary, or longer than applicable law.
* We ask that you not include any sensitive information in your emails to us. Sensitive information includes information that, if exposed, could result in harm, discrimination, or severe consequences for an individual. Examples include login credentials, Social Security numbers, information related to racial or ethnic origin, health and genetic data, political opinions, sexual orientation, religious beliefs and views, criminal backgrounds, trade union membership, and financial information. We will never ask you for any sensitive information, and we do not need any to provide or improve our Services. If we believe you have sent us any sensitive information, we will inform you that you have and immediately delete it from our account so that we cannot access it.

In the event of a security incident involving us that could seriously impact you, including unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of your information, loss of information, or other breach of privacy, we will notify you and the proper authorities as soon as possible.<br/><br/>

**Children's Privacy**\
Because our intended audience for our game is teens and adults, and to comply with Meta Horizon platform requirements, we have utilized Meta’s Age Group Self-certification process to set our game’s age group to teens and adults. As a result, our Services are not intended for children, who, under the United States’ Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), are those under 13, and under the European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), those under 16.

In following Meta’s Content Guidelines, we do not permit children under the age of 10 to use our Services. Children who are between 10 and 12 are also not permitted to access our Services, as we have self-certified our game for teens and adults (13+).

If you are between 13 and 16 years of age and your parent or legal guardian allows you to, you may play our game, but do not contact us or send us any information about yourself, which includes your name, email address, phone number, or location. Individuals under 16 are not allowed to provide any personal or sensitive information, and we do not intentionally or knowingly use, collect, process, or disclose information that may be used to identify, contact, or track individuals under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided us with any personal or sensitive information, please contact us so that we can immediately delete any information we have about them.<br/>

## 3. How Our Other Third Parties Handle Data
Marbalance VR was built using the Unity game engine and for Meta Quest headsets, so we utilize Unity Technologies and Meta Quest hardware, software, or tools.

**Unity Technologies**\
Unity Technologies offers [several services](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy#:~:text=Services%20Provided%20Include) that may process your personal information when implemented in the games you play. However, we do not utilize or implement any of these services in our game.


**Meta**\
Meta offers [several platform features](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/spatial-sdk/ps-platform-intro/#list-of-platform-features) that may process your personal information when integrated into games you play. We have implemented a Meta platform feature in our game that may use your Meta profile’s user ID information. Your user ID helps determine if you have legitimate access to our game by cross-referencing specific application, user, and merchant data. This process occurs when you start playing our game, and only between your device and Meta, so we do not receive your Meta profile’s user ID information or any other information.

For a more detailed explanation of how the third-party entities we use collect and handle data, please view their privacy policies through the links below:
* [Unity Technologies Privacy Policy](https://unity3d.com/legal/privacy-policy)
* [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/)
* [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en-US)

These privacy policies may change from time to time, so please review them for any updates to how they collect and handle data.<br/>
